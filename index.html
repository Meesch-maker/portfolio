Desktop app · built with Claude

Projection mapper

The app behind the projection work above. Its auto space-mapping algorithm maps a whole room's surfaces in about 30 seconds instead of fitting each one by hand, and the visuals react live to audio. Mesh warps, masks and blend modes are there for fine-tuning by hand.

  • Auto mapping in 30 s
  • Audio-reactive visuals
  • Mesh warp
  • Masks & blend modes

A chord-progression builder for composing. Build progressions of up to 16 chords on a circle of fifths, Tonnetz or chromatic clock, add colour and bass notes, then export MIDI straight into Ableton.
